# Cabin Spark design direction

## Subject, audience, and job

Mobile detailing for Houston drivers focused on the cabin surfaces they touch and see every day. The page should make interior and whole-vehicle care easy to understand and lead to calling or booking online once contact details are confirmed.

## Visual system

- Cabin graphite — `#1E2027`
- Console slate — `#343946`
- Upholstery silver — `#E1E6EA`
- Glass white — `#FAFBFC`
- Spark amber — `#F6B73C`
- Display: Archivo Black
- Body: Manrope
- Utility: IBM Plex Mono

## Layout thesis

The precision frame works like a cabin inspection lamp, concentrating attention on high-touch surfaces before expanding to paint, glass, and wheels.

## Signature

A four-point spark sits inside a cabin outline and marks inspection moments without becoming a glow effect.

## Critique

The first concept looked like an electrical service brand. The revision keeps the spark small and grounds it in an unmistakable cabin frame.
